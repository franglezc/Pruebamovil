## Brand & Style

The visual identity embodies the spirit of modern mechanical engineering, technical rigor, and automotive craftsmanship. Built for clients who demand absolute precision, trust, and transparency in vehicle maintenance, the aesthetic merges technical minimalism with high-contrast industrial elegance. 

The aesthetic tone is dark, focused, and purposeful:
- **Style Archetype:** Industrial Technical Minimalism with High-Contrast Monochromatic Accents.
- **Atmosphere:** Clean workshop floors, brushed metallic finishes, laser diagnostics, and precision telemetry displays.
- **Target Audience:** Vehicle owners, performance enthusiasts, and fleet managers seeking a premium, professional automotive service experience.
- **Emotional Impact:** Reassurance, engineered competence, unwavering reliability, and uncompromising clarity.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from the dark matte graphite chassis and crisp white linear silhouette of the brand mark. The foundation relies on pitch graphite surfaces, deep carbon tiering, and high-luminance white for typography and iconography to maintain pristine legibility under any lighting conditions.

- **Primary Canvas & Neutral (`#121316`):** Deep matte graphite/carbon. Avoid absolute `#000000` to prevent visual fatigue while keeping contrast sharp and grounded.
- **Surface Elevation Steps:**
  - Base Background: `#121316`
  - Elevated Container / Cards: `#1A1C20`
  - Interactive / Active Elements: `#242830`
  - Subtle Dividing Line: `#2D323B`
- **Primary Accent (`#FFFFFF`):** High-emission pure white, serving as the dominant focus for titles, key metrics, and primary action affordances.
- **Secondary Accent (`#94A3B8`):** Cool slate silver representing machined aluminum, used for secondary copy, captions, and structural outlines.
- **Tertiary Diagnostic Accent (`#38BDF8`):** An electric telemetry cyan used sparingly for system indicators, live statuses, and vehicle diagnostics warnings without compromising the monochrome sophistication.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an interplay between automotive engineering and digital precision:
- **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Displays mechanical structure, clean geometric apexes, and aerodynamic curvature reflecting the automotive profile of the brand symbol.
- **Body (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Highly legible, neutral grotesque optimized for technical inspection reports, invoices, and service checklists.
- **Labels & Telemetry (`JetBrains Mono`):** Monospaced type used for vehicle identification numbers (VIN), part references, mileage counters, diagnostic codes, and timestamps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a structured 12-column modular grid for desktop environments (scaling to a 4-column layout on mobile) that replicates technical shop manuals and engineering spec sheets.

- **Rhythm & Grid:** A base 8px increment system enforces disciplined vertical and horizontal cadence.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - Mobile: `< 640px` (single column cards, full-width diagnostic rows, compact margins).
  - Tablet: `640px – 1024px` (6-column split layout, dual-column service breakdowns).
  - Desktop: `> 1024px` (12-column system, max container width of 1280px to maintain reading focus).
- **Whitespace Discipline:** High density in operational tables and telemetry metrics, paired with generous macro whitespace between structural modules to reinforce premium service authority.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is conveyed through tonal surface tiers and low-contrast perimeter rules rather than heavy diffused drop shadows, maintaining an authentic brushed-matte feel.

- **Base Canvas:** The deepest floor level sits at `#121316`.
- **Level 1 (Panels & Cards):** Raised to `#1A1C20` with a 1px perimeter border rendered in `#2D323B`.
- **Level 2 (Active States, Dialogs, Menus):** Sits at `#242830` with a crisp outer edge `#3E4552`.
- **Outlines over Shadows:** Avoid colored or diffuse blur drop-shadows. Elevation relies on subtle, razor-sharp outlines (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) simulating precisely machined tolerances.

## Shapes

The shape language reflects engineered components: clean, calibrated, and subtly beveled. 

- **Corner Radius:** Standard components feature `0.25rem` (4px) micro-rounding, avoiding playful curves in favor of industrial precision.
- **Containers & Modals:** Use `0.5rem` (8px) for cards, dialogs, and service blocks.
- **Pill Forms:** Reserved exclusively for vehicle status tags (e.g., "EN REVISIÓN", "COMPLETADO", "ITV FAVORABLE").

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary:* Solid `#FFFFFF` background with `#121316` bold text. Instant high contrast, razor-sharp 4px radius.
  - *Secondary:* Transparent fill with a 1px `#FFFFFF` outline and `#FFFFFF` text. Subtle hover fills to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
  - *Tertiary / Ghost:* Borderless, slate-colored `#94A3B8` text, shifting to `#FFFFFF` on hover.

- **Data Cards & Inspection Modules:**
  - Background `#1A1C20`, 1px border `#2D323B`.
  - Headers flanked by technical monospaced indices (e.g., `SEC_01 // MOTOR`).

- **Input Fields:**
  - Dark container `#14161A` recessed beneath a 1px border in `#2D323B`.
  - Labels positioned above in `JetBrains Mono` uppercase. Active focus switches border to `#FFFFFF` with no chromatic glow.

- **Chips & Status Indicators:**
  - Compact badges with `JetBrains Mono` styling.
  - Success and active indicators utilize solid high-contrast borders and telemetry cyan dots (`#38BDF8`).

- **Checkboxes & Radios:**
  - Crisp square profiles (2px corner radius) with `#2D323B` borders. Selected states snap to `#FFFFFF` fill with an inverted `#121316` checkmark.

- **Service Timeline / Diagnostic Stepper:**
  - Monochromatic vertical rule connecting work stages (Diagnóstico, Presupuesto, Reparación, Control de Calidad, Entrega), with completed stages highlighted in high-contrast `#FFFFFF`.
